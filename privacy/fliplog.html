Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Enigma Machine Dev ("we," "us," or "our") operates the FlipLog mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

Overview

FlipLog is a resale and flipping tracker designed with your privacy in mind. All data you create in FlipLog is stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal information or app data. The App has no accounts, no cloud sync, and no telemetry.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not connect to any server, and does not send any data over the internet.

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Inventory items you track (titles, purchase prices, costs, sale details, notes)
  • Deal evaluations and offer histories you record
  • Comparable sale prices you enter manually
  • Photos you attach to items (stored in your device's local storage)
  • Platform fee configurations and financial settings
  • App settings and preferences

This data is stored using your device's local storage (MMKV) and never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

Photos and Camera

FlipLog allows you to attach photos to inventory items. The App may request access to your device's camera and photo library for this purpose. Photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server. Granting camera or photo library access is optional — all other App features work without it.

Data Export and Import

FlipLog allows you to export your data as a JSON or CSV file and import it again later. These files are created and managed entirely by you. We have no access to any files you export or import. Exports are initiated only by your explicit action and contain only the data you have entered into the App.

Permissions

FlipLog may request the following permissions:

  • Camera — Optional. Used only when you choose to take a photo to attach to an item. No photos are transmitted anywhere.
  • Read Media Images / Photo Library — Optional. Used only when you choose to attach an existing photo from your gallery to an item. No photos are transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

FlipLog does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. No third party receives any data about your use of the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

All your data lives on your device. You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data, or by using the clear data option in your device's application settings.

GDPR Rights (European Economic Area)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, most GDPR rights are satisfied by design. All data is under your direct control on your own device. You may exercise your right to erasure by uninstalling the App or clearing its data.

CCPA Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for us to disclose, sell, or delete on our end. All data is stored locally on your device under your control.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we collect no personal data at all, no such data could be collected from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy at this URL and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
