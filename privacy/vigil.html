Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Enigma Machine Dev ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Vigil mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

Overview

Vigil is an alarm and timer app designed with your privacy in mind. All data you create in Vigil is stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your personal information or app data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not connect to any server, and does not send any data over the internet.

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Alarm configurations (times, labels, sounds, snooze settings)
  • Timer and stopwatch definitions
  • Presets and weekly schedules
  • Day overrides and customizations
  • Rules and rule-based alarm configurations
  • App settings and preferences

This data is stored in local files on your device and never leaves your device.

Permissions

Vigil requests the following Android permissions to deliver its core alarm functionality. All permissions are used solely on your device and do not involve transmitting data to any server.

  • Schedule Exact Alarm / Use Exact Alarm — Required to fire alarms at the precise times you set, bypassing Android's standard battery-saving timer restrictions.
  • Receive Boot Completed — Required to reschedule your alarms automatically after your device restarts, so alarms are never missed following a reboot.
  • Vibrate — Required to vibrate your device when an alarm fires.
  • Wake Lock — Required to keep your device awake while an alarm is ringing so it can be dismissed properly.
  • Use Full Screen Intent — Required to display the alarm screen over your lock screen when your device is locked.
  • Post Notifications / Access Notification Policy — Required to show alarm and timer notifications in your notification shade.
  • Foreground Service / Foreground Service Media Playback — Required to play alarm audio reliably, even when the app is in the background.

Third-Party Services

Vigil does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. No third party receives any data about your use of the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

All your data lives on your device. You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data, or by using the clear data option in your device's application settings.

GDPR Rights (European Economic Area)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, most GDPR rights are satisfied by design. All data is under your direct control on your own device. You may exercise your right to erasure by uninstalling the App or clearing its data.

CCPA Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for us to disclose, sell, or delete on our end. All data is stored locally on your device under your control.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we collect no personal data at all, no such data could be collected from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy at this URL and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
